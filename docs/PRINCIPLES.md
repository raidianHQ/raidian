Purpose:
Establishes the enduring principles that shape every feature, interaction, architectural decision, and user experience within the Raidian platform, ensuring the project remains faithful to its mission and values as it evolves.

**Audience:**

Project owners, contributors, designers, software engineers, AI development agents, and anyone making decisions that influence the behavior, architecture, user experience, or future direction of the Raidian platform.

---

# Core Product Principles

## Reflection Over Prediction

Raidian exists to encourage thoughtful reflection rather than claims about future events.

---

## User Agency Over Dependence

Users remain responsible for their own decisions.

The application should never encourage emotional dependence upon itself.

---

## Humility Over Certainty

Interpretations are invitations for consideration.

They are never presented as unquestionable truth.

---

## Transparency Over Mystery

The application should clearly distinguish between symbolic interpretation, AI-generated content, factual information, and optional biblical encouragement.

---

## Wisdom Over Fear

Fear is never an acceptable design strategy.

The application should encourage courage, hope, honesty, compassion, and thoughtful self-examination.

---

## Privacy By Design

Privacy is treated as a core product feature.

Users should retain ownership and control of their personal reflections.

---

## Respect For Diverse Beliefs

Users may come from many different philosophical and religious backgrounds.

The application should respect those differences while remaining honest about its own design philosophy.

---

## Simplicity Over Complexity

Every screen should have a clear purpose.

Every feature should justify its existence.

Every interaction should feel calm, intentional, and understandable.

---

## Documentation Before Complexity

If something becomes difficult to explain, it is probably too complicated.

Architecture should remain understandable by future developers.

---

## Build For Decades

Short-term convenience should never undermine long-term maintainability.
