# Raidian

*A guided reflection platform for thoughtful self-discovery.*

---

## Overview

Raidian is a personal reflection platform that uses symbolic systems, artificial intelligence, journaling, and optional Scripture to encourage thoughtful self-examination and personal growth.

The project is built on the belief that symbols can serve as mirrors for reflection rather than predictors of the future.

Raidian is designed to help users ask better questions, explore meaningful insights, and maintain a personal record of their journey over time.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

- Reflection over prediction
- User agency over dependence
- Humility over certainty
- Privacy by design
- Wisdom over fear
- Long-term maintainability
